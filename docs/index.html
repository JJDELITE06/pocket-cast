Before you download

This is an experimental release. The app compiles and native receiver checks pass, but live iPhone mirroring has not yet been verified. The updated USB interface also needs live testing. Read the compatibility notes below before relying on it.

A simple start.

No iPhone app or account is required. The Windows download includes the receiver and playback components.

  1. Extract and open

    Extract the entire ZIP, then open PocketCast.exe. Keep the included runtime folder beside the app.

  2. Choose a connection

    For Wi-Fi, connect both devices to the same local network and click Start receiver. USB tethering needs the setup described below.

  3. Select your PC

    On iPhone, open Control Center → Screen Mirroring and choose Pocket Cast. Enter the PIN shown in the app if asked.

Find your connection.

Both modes use AirPlay. USB tethering carries that local connection through the cable; it requires more than simply plugging in the phone.

Wi-Fi / Ethernet

  • Put the iPhone and PC on the same local network.
  • The PC can use Wi-Fi or Ethernet.
  • Allow the bundled receiver through Windows Firewall on your trusted network.

School, office, hotel, or guest Wi-Fi may separate devices or block discovery. The same Wi-Fi name does not guarantee that devices can find each other.

USB tethering

  • Use a data-capable USB cable and trust the PC on your iPhone.
  • Enable iPhone Personal Hotspot.
  • Windows must detect a working Apple Mobile Device Ethernet connection.

Select USB tethering in Pocket Cast, keep the cable attached, and follow USB setup in the app. Your carrier must support Personal Hotspot; other PC traffic may use cellular data.

Windows on ARM: a compatible ARM64 iPhone network driver is required. Apple Devices may recognize the phone without providing USB Ethernet. USB mode cannot start without that connection.

What your PC needs

Windows 10 or 11, .NET Framework 4.8 or newer, a Direct3D 11-capable GPU, and a working audio output. The receiver is x64; Windows 11 ARM can run it using x64 emulation, subject to driver availability.

Picture quality

Maximum mode requests up to 4K at 60 fps with HEVC. Actual resolution and frame rate depend on your iPhone, content, PC, and connection. AirPlay compresses video; lossless mirroring, native resolution, HDR, and 120 fps are not guaranteed.

Why does Pocket Cast not appear on my iPhone?

Confirm the receiver is started and both devices can communicate on the same local network. VPN routing, firewall rules, guest-network isolation, or separate network segments can block discovery. Restart the receiver after changing connections.

Where do the picture and audio appear?

The iPhone picture opens in a separate, resizable video window. Audio plays through the PC’s default speakers or headphones. Stop from the app or your iPhone’s Screen Mirroring menu. This receiver does not remotely control the phone.

Will every app, microphone, or phone call be shared?

No. Protected streaming apps may block mirroring, and microphone or call audio is not guaranteed. Live compatibility and quality still need testing with your iPhone.

What has been tested?

The frontend compiles, its WPF markup has been parsed, and native component checks cover discovery, receiver responses, network interface selection, and audio/video playback components. Live iPhone screen sharing and USB mirroring have not been verified. The new frontend’s execution tests were blocked by Windows Application Control on the development machine. The download includes a detailed validation record.
